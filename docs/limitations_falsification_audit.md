# Limitations & falsification audit

## Falsification conditions stated in the manuscript (§8)
1. Norm persistence after carrier recession is sufficiently explained by non-religious pathways.
2. Putatively released norms turn out to depend covertly on the representation's continued salience.
3. Post-recession persistence proves short-lived residue rather than cross-generational reproduction.
4. Release cannot be distinguished empirically from ordinary secularisation.
5. Norm renewal needs no reference to the religious scaffold's history.
6. The revival account collapses into existing heritage/lived-religion theory.

## Self-check against spec §25 quality control
- Release ≠ renamed secularisation: §4 lists distinguishing conditions (i–iii) and separates release from secularisation explicitly.
- Anscombe handled frontally: §4 subsection; completion vs incoherence readings both kept open; no denial of her worry.
- Deus otiosus distinguished: god-level withdrawal vs norm-level, function-specific independence.
- Vestigialisation distinguished: inversion of what survives (structure vs norm); complementarity asserted, not rivalry.
- Individual residue vs cultural transmission not conflated: separate paragraphs; residue = mechanism evidence at individual level only.
- Personification causality hedged: "suggestive support… no clean demonstration" (§2).
- Cycle is norm-specific: stated in §6 and Figure 1 caption note.
- Paper 2 content: single deferred sentence on measurement/legibility.
- Paper 3 content: release-vs-perpetual-authority posed as a question only; no One–Many mechanics.
- "Carry It Without Me" tied to the definition (§4) and returned to in Conclusion as interpretive metaphor.
- Warm-watching deity confined to Discussion end, marked non-empirical.
- Conclusion does not revert to generic decline talk.

## Known soft spots (for reviewers' anticipated objections)
- Cross-generational independence evidence is illustrative, not demonstrated; the paper's own condition (iii) is the hardest to meet empirically.
- Kappa→mascot could be read as vestigialisation rather than release; the manuscript presents it as diagnostic ambiguity, which is honest but invites the counter-reading.
- "Norm-specific" cycles still need operationalisation for empirical work (what counts as the same norm across contexts).
